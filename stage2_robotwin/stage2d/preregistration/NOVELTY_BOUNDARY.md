# Novelty boundary

This stage tests a mechanism, not a new model family.  Its potentially distinct unit
is the combination of (a) same-state donor-fade counterfactuals that estimate future
takeover capacity, (b) an independently generated desired-responsibility state
machine, and (c) constrained 4D allocation that preserves net effect while changing
who supplies it.  Shapley/current-contribution routing, phase ramps, contact duration,
force thresholds, release guards, conservation-only allocation, swapped/shuffled/
time-shifted targets, and operator-null are explicit comparators.

The evidence may support only a bounded oracle mechanism on the frozen task.  It does
not establish deployability, learned estimation, policy improvement, cross-task
generalization, or an N3 novelty grade.  Two independent reviewers must assess this
boundary after preregistration and before interpretation of final results.
