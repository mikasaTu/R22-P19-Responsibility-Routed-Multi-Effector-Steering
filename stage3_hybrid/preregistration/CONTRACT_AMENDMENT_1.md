# Execution amendment 1

Frozen before calibration completion and before any eligibility aggregation.
No registered threshold or mechanism changes.

If more than two single-factor conditions satisfy all four calibration
eligibility criteria, the held-out matrix uses exactly the lexicographically
first two condition names. This rule does not inspect gain magnitude,
disturbance, held-out outcomes, or mode identity. If fewer than two are
eligible, the original `NO_INFORMATIVE_FAILURE_SPACE` decision applies and no
held-out/ranker experiment is allowed.
