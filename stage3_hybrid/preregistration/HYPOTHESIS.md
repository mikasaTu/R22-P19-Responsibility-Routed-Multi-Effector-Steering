# Frozen Stage3A hypothesis

Status: `accepted: false`.

The only claims under test are: (1) selecting one of six discrete, paired donor
arm-and-gripper time-warp modes can improve eventual handover outcomes relative
to exact replay under an informative stress; and, only after that upper bound is
established, (2) a transparent bounded-horizon consequence model can recover at
least 70% of that oracle gain without outcome leakage.

This is not evidence for continuous responsibility allocation, a learned VLA,
or deployable routing. Historical Stage2 negative decisions remain unchanged.
